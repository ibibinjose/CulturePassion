**FY27 (inaugural year) · Melbourne**

## 1. What we are offering, plainly

CulturePass is the cultural participation layer for multicultural cities. The Culture Passion Awards are its recognition program: real money and real recognition for the people who keep cultural life running — elders preserving endangered practices, volunteer festival organisers, young innovators, cross-community collaborations.

**What a sponsor gets that they cannot buy elsewhere: measured reach into specific cultural communities, with evidence of who actually participated.**

Not impressions. Not estimated audience. Verified attendance at real cultural events, segmented by community and suburb, from signed check-ins at the door.

## 2. Why this is different from ordinary event sponsorship

| Ordinary cultural sponsorship | CulturePass |
|---|---|
| One festival, one weekend, one community | Hundreds of events across 45+ cultural communities, year-round |
| Attendance estimated by the organiser | Verified check-ins with signed digital passes |
| Audience composition unknown | Composition reported by community and suburb, aggregate and privacy-protected |
| Logo on a banner | Native placement in a discovery product people actually open |
| No measurement after the event | Monthly performance reporting from platform data |
| Reach is whoever came | Reach is whoever came, plus everyone who browsed |

**The honest version:** we are pre-launch. There is no audience yet. What we are selling in FY27 is founding-partner position in the inaugural year of a program and a platform, at inaugural-year pricing, with the measurement infrastructure already built rather than promised.

## 5. What sponsors receive in reporting

Monthly, from platform data, with a quarterly review:

| Metric | Detail |
|---|---|
| Reach | Impressions and engaged views on sponsored placements |
| Attendance | Verified check-ins at events in your sponsored collection or category |
| Community composition | Which cultural communities participated, in aggregate |
| Geographic dispersal | Suburb-level participation |
| Sentiment and earned media | Coverage of the awards and your association with it |
| Awards program | Nominations, votes, media reach, recipient outcomes |

**Privacy floor, and it is not negotiable:** all reporting is aggregate with small-cell suppression (no cell below 5). **Individual-level data is never shared with sponsors.** No exception, no premium tier, no price. If a prospective partner requires individual-level targeting data, we are not the right platform for them — and we will say so in the first meeting rather than the fifth.

## 6. The rules a sponsor is agreeing to

These are in every contract. They are not boilerplate — they are the reason the platform is worth sponsoring, and a partner who cannot accept them is disqualified.

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| **Sponsored placement is labelled and does not influence organic ranking.** "For You" is not purchasable. | The feed's credibility is the asset. A feed people distrust reaches nobody. |
| **Sponsors have no vote, no veto and no advance sight of award recipients.** | An awards program with contested independence is worth less than none. |
| **Prize money is paid directly to recipients.** No sponsor-branded handover as a condition. | The award belongs to the recipient. |
| **Cultural imagery in any activation requires a partnering community host and Cultural Advisory Council review.** | Cultural material is not creative inventory. |
| **No individual-level data.** | Stated above. Permanent. |
| **Free-RSVP events never attract a fee, and sponsorship does not change that.** | Trust position. |

## 7. Target sponsor categories, and the honest fit assessment

| Category | Why they fit | What they will ask that we cannot give |
|---|---|---|
| **Banking & payments** | Diaspora customer acquisition; strong existing multicultural marketing budgets | Individual-level targeting data |
| **Money transfer / remittance** | Almost perfect audience alignment | Individual-level targeting; may want lead capture |
| **Telecommunications** | Migrant and student segments are core to their growth | Behavioural data at user level |
| **Airlines** | Diaspora travel is a large, well-understood segment | Direct booking attribution |
| **Grocery & FMCG with diaspora brands** | Direct product relevance, local activation | Store-level attribution |
| **Universities** | International student recruitment and retention; belonging outcomes | Student-level identification |
| **Health insurers** | Migrant onboarding is a major acquisition channel | Individual health-adjacent data — a hard no |
| **State tourism bodies** | Dispersal and visitor participation data is genuinely valuable to them | Nothing problematic. **Best-fit category.** |
| **Local government** | Participation measurement they cannot otherwise obtain | Nothing problematic. Buys the civic licence rather than sponsorship. |

**Deliberately not approached:** gambling, tobacco, alcohol brands as presenting partner (category partner considered case by case with Cultural Advisory Council input, given that many cultural communities on the platform are abstinent for religious reasons), payday and short-term lending, and any brand with an active dispute with a community represented on the platform.

## 8. What we are asking for, and what we are not claiming

**The ask:** a 20-minute conversation, and if there is a fit, a founding-partner position in the inaugural year.

**What is true today:** the platform is built and working — 39 screens, production AWS infrastructure, signed-QR check-in, first-party analytics. The awards program is fully designed, governed, and budgeted. The Melbourne pilot launches September 2026.

**What is not true today, stated because you will find out anyway:** there is no audience yet. No paying customers, no signed sponsors, no launched pilot. Every audience figure in this document is a planning projection from an explicit model, available on request in full, including its downside cases.

FY27 sponsorship is priced as inaugural-year risk, and it should be evaluated as such. A partner who wants proven reach should talk to us in FY28. A partner who wants the founding position — and the association with the first cohort of recipients — can only get it now.
