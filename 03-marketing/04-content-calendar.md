# CulturePass — Content & Campaign Calendar (FY27)

**Owner: Growth & Content Marketer** · Reviewed monthly, re-planned quarterly.
FY27 = September 2026 – August 2027.

---

## 1. The organising principle

**The cultural calendar is the content calendar.** We do not invent campaign moments; the communities on the platform already have them, and they are the moments when intent to participate is highest.

Two consequences:
1. Campaign planning starts from the festival calendar, working backwards 4–6 weeks.
2. Several major festivals move each year (lunar and Islamic calendars). **Every date below marked ⚠ must be verified against the current year's calendar before planning.** Getting a festival date wrong is not a scheduling error in this category — it is a credibility error, and communities notice immediately.

## 3. Content pillars and weekly cadence

| Pillar | Share | Purpose | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| **What's on** | 40% | Direct acquisition and retention | Weekly digest, suburb pages, festival guides, collections |
| **Host stories** | 25% | Trust, host acquisition, earned media | Profiles of real organisers, named and credited |
| **How-to for organisers** | 15% | Host acquisition and activation | Publishing guides, ticketing guides, grant-application help |
| **Community context** | 10% | Cultural depth and SEO | "What is Onam", festival explainers — **written or reviewed by someone from that community, paid** |
| **Platform & impact** | 10% | Investor, funder and civic audiences | Impact reporting, product updates, data stories |

### Weekly rhythm

| Day | Output | Channel |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | Host story or how-to published | Blog, LinkedIn, newsletter |
| Tuesday | Suburb spotlight | Instagram, Facebook |
| Wednesday | Community context piece or festival explainer | Blog, SEO |
| **Thursday 17:00** | **Weekly personalised digest** | **Email — the single most important retention asset** |
| Friday | This weekend collection | Social, in-app |
| Saturday–Sunday | Live event content, host reshares | Stories |

Thursday 17:00 because cultural events cluster Friday–Sunday and the decision gets made Thursday evening. This has been chosen deliberately and should be A/B tested, not assumed.

## 5. Campaign templates

### Suburb launch (2 weeks)

| Timing | Action |
|---|---|
| T-14d | Suburb page live with every event on the platform there |
| T-10d | All hosts in the suburb briefed on the coordinated share weekend |
| T-7d | QR posters distributed: grocery shops, halls, libraries, places of worship, cafés |
| T-5d | Council newsletter and community radio placements confirmed |
| T-3d | Local business offers live |
| T-0 | **Coordinated host share weekend** — every host posts their own event to their own list on the same weekend |
| T+3d | Retargeting to page visitors who did not register |
| T+7d | Local media follow-up with a named host story |
| T+14d | Review: registrations, activation, cost per participant. Feeds the next suburb. |

### Festival campaign (6 weeks)

| Timing | Action |
|---|---|
| T-6w | Host outreach: "are you running something for [festival]?" — this is also a supply-acquisition moment |
| T-5w | Festival collection page live; explainer content published (community-authored, paid) |
| T-4w | In-language community media briefed |
| T-3w | Digest feature; followed-community alerts |
| T-2w | Paid retargeting begins; host coordinated shares |
| T-1w | "This week" push; reminders |
| T-0 | Live coverage, host reshares, on-the-ground content with permission |
| T+1w | Recap; attendance data to hosts; next festival teased |

## 7. Production capacity, honestly

The FY27 plan above assumes **one full-time growth marketer plus contracted translation and photography.** That is roughly:

- 1 host story/week (interview + write + shoot) — 1 day
- 3 SEO pieces/week — 1.5 days
- Weekly digest curation and send — 0.5 day
- Social production — 1 day
- Campaign management — 1 day

That is 5 days of work in a 5-day week with zero slack, which means it will slip. Explicit prioritisation when it does, in this order:

1. **Weekly digest** — never skipped. It is the retention engine.
2. **Suburb and festival campaigns** — tied to acquisition targets and to the pilot gates.
3. **Host stories** — tied to earned media and host acquisition.
4. **SEO volume** — reduce quantity before quality. Three good pages beat nine thin ones.
5. **Social frequency** — first thing to cut. It is the least measurable.

Planning content capacity we do not have is how content calendars become fiction by month three. Better to plan four channels well.
