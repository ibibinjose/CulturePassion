## G3 — State cultural or multicultural body

**Subject:** `Cultural participation measurement — [State body]`

> Dear [First name / Title],
>
> I'm writing to introduce CulturePass and to ask whether a conversation would be useful.
>
> CulturePass is a platform that makes cultural events discoverable and cultural participation measurable. Community organisations publish free; residents find events in their own language; and attendance is verified at the door rather than estimated afterwards. It is built and operating, and launches in Melbourne in September 2026.
>
> The reason I'm writing to [body] specifically: your funded organisations report participation to you, and that reporting is necessarily self-estimated. There is currently no instrument that measures cultural participation at community granularity — which means the question "is participation growing, or are we reaching the same people repeatedly?" cannot be answered in any Australian jurisdiction.
>
> Our platform produces that data as a by-product of ordinary use: attendance by cultural community, cultural and linguistic representation relative to population, geographic dispersal, and revenue generated for small hosts. Individual-level data is never shared — all reporting is aggregate with small-cell suppression.
>
> I'd value 30 minutes to understand your priorities and whether this is relevant to them. I'm not asking for funding in this email.
>
> Sincerely,
>
> **Bibin Jose** · Founder & CEO, CulturePass

**Notes.** Explicitly not asking for money in a first approach to a funding body is a deliberate and effective choice. It makes the meeting easy to accept and it means the funding conversation, when it comes, is between people who already know each other.

## G5 — Auspice partner request

**Subject:** `Auspice partnership — cultural participation project`

> Dear [First name],
>
> I'm writing with a specific request, and an offer that stands regardless of your answer.
>
> **The offer, unconditionally:** CulturePass launches in Melbourne this September. We'd like to give [organisation] and all your member organisations free Professional-tier access permanently, plus a co-branded collection page and aggregate participation reporting you can use in your own funding and advocacy. No cost, no conditions.
>
> **The request:** we're applying to [funder], which requires a DGR-endorsed applicant. As a proprietary company we're not eligible. Would [organisation] consider auspicing the application? We'd propose the standard administration fee, and CulturePass delivers the project work while you hold the funds and the acquittal obligation.
>
> The project is onboarding and digital capability support for cultural organisations in [area], delivered in nine languages — work that aligns closely with what [organisation] already does.
>
> I'd welcome a conversation about either or both. The free access offer stands whatever you decide about auspicing.
>
> **Bibin Jose** · Founder & CEO, CulturePass

**Notes.** Separating the unconditional offer from the request is deliberate and it matters. It makes clear you are not trading access for a favour, which is how an auspice request can otherwise read.
