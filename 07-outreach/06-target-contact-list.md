# CulturePass — Outreach Target List (FY27)

**Owner: CEO** · Working list for the FY27 pilot year.

> ## ⚠ ORGANISATIONS, NOT PEOPLE
> This list deliberately names **organisations and the role titles to look for** — not individuals. Naming a specific person from memory is how you email someone who left eighteen months ago, which is the most avoidable own goal in outreach.
>
> **Before any send:** find the current holder of the role, verify it on LinkedIn or the organisation's own site, and record the date you verified it. Also verify that each organisation still exists and operates as described — community sector organisations merge, rename and close regularly.

## 4. Wave 4 — Sponsors (Oct–Dec 2026)

| Category | Target types | Role to find | Package |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Money transfer / remittance** | Major remittance providers operating in AU | Head of Marketing, or Community/Diaspora Marketing | Presenting or Category |
| **Banking** | Major AU banks with multicultural banking segments | Multicultural Marketing; Community Partnerships; Foundation lead | Presenting or Category |
| **Telco** | Major AU telcos and MVNOs targeting migrant segments | Brand Partnerships; Multicultural Marketing | Category |
| **Airlines** | Carriers with strong AU–South Asia, AU–Middle East, AU–Southeast Asia routes | Sponsorship / Brand Partnerships | Category |
| **Grocery / FMCG** | Supermarket chains; diaspora food brands and importers | Brand or Community Marketing | City Partner |
| **Universities** | International-recruitment-heavy institutions | International Marketing; Student Experience | City Partner |
| **Health insurance** | Insurers with overseas-student and visitor cover products | Community/Multicultural Marketing | Category — note the hard no on individual health-adjacent data |
| **State tourism** | Visit Victoria | Partnerships; Events | **Best structural fit — dispersal data is genuinely valuable to them** |

**Excluded by policy:** gambling, tobacco, alcohol as presenting partner (category considered case-by-case with Cultural Advisory Council input, given many communities on the platform are abstinent for religious reasons), payday and short-term lending, and any brand in active dispute with a community on the platform.

**Qualification rule:** if you cannot find a **published** DEI, CSR or community commitment to cite, do not send. That is the personalisation bar from the playbook, and an unpersonalised sponsorship email is worse than none.

**Do not open this wave until the pilot has 60+ days of data.**

| Tier | Target type | How to reach |
|---|---|---|

| 1 | Impact and civic-tech funds (AU and regional) | Warm intro; lead with the measurement layer |

## 7. Weekly discipline

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| **Verify the contact's current role before every send, and log the date** | The single most common own goal in outreach |
| Personalise to the playbook's minimum bar, or do not send | An unpersonalised template is worse than silence |
| Respect the touch limits: 3 for sponsors, 4 for community, unlimited-but-quarterly for councils | Over-chasing costs the next conversation |
| Log every objection verbatim | Three identical objections means the pitch is wrong, not the prospects |
| Reply to any inbound within 24 hours | Non-negotiable |
| Written summary within 24 hours of every meeting, with a dated next step | Outreach fails after the positive reply, not before it |
| Never automate community outreach | It is recognised instantly and it is unrecoverable |
