# READY-TO-SEND DRAFTS — NOTHING HAS BEEN SENT

**Status as at 21 August 2026: zero emails sent. Zero contacts made. Zero commitments given.**

Everything in this folder is a draft prepared for your review and your signature. No message has gone to any sponsor, funder, investor, council or community organisation, and none will without you sending it yourself.

---

## Why nothing was sent

Three reasons, all of them the right call:

1. **Sending is yours to do.** Outreach to funders, sponsors and communities creates commitments and first impressions in your name. That is not a task to delegate to an automated process.
2. **Every draft has gaps only you can fill.** Recipient names, verified roles, the specific personalisation that makes each email worth sending, your phone number.
3. **Some claims need checking first.** Several drafts reference pilot metrics that do not exist yet. Sending them today would state things that are not true.

---

## Before you send anything

| Step | Detail |
|---|---|
| 1. Incorporate | Several drafts reference the company. See [`../../06-funding/08-entity-and-foundation-strategy.md`](../../06-funding/08-entity-and-foundation-strategy.md) — this is blocking for investor and grant outreach. |
| 2. Set up a domain email | `bibin@culturepass.app` rather than a Gmail address. Materially more credible for government and corporate recipients. Do this before Wave 3. |
| 3. Fill every `[bracket]` | An unfilled bracket that goes out is unrecoverable |
| 4. Verify each recipient's current role | On LinkedIn or the organisation's own site. Record the date. |
| 5. Check the claims | Nothing may state a host, attendance or revenue figure that does not exist. See [`../01-outreach-playbook.md`](../01-outreach-playbook.md) §2. |
| 6. Personalise | To the minimum bar in the playbook §4. If you cannot, the prospect is not researched enough to contact. |
| 7. Send in wave order | Community partners → councils → grants → sponsors → investors. The order is the strategy. |
