# Outreach Emails — Sponsors & Corporate Partners

**Status: DRAFTS. Nothing sent.** Fill every `[bracket]`, verify the recipient's current role, and send from your own account.
Cadence: 3 touches, then stop. See [`01-outreach-playbook.md`](01-outreach-playbook.md) §3.

---

## S1 — Cold outreach, presenting partner (primary template)

**Subject:** `Culture Passion Awards — founding partner, [Brand]`

> Dear [First name],
>
> I'm reaching out from CulturePass, a platform launching in Melbourne this September that makes cultural events findable — from a Malayalam theatre night in Footscray to a Collingwood warehouse exhibition. Community organisations publish for free; residents find events near them in their own language.
>
> I'm writing because of [Brand]'s [name the specific, published commitment — e.g. "Multicultural Australia program" / "community grants in Western Sydney"]. [One sentence on why it genuinely connects to what we do.]
>
> In 2027 we're launching the **Culture Passion Awards** — unrestricted grants and recognition for people who keep cultural life running and have almost never been recognised for it. Elders preserving endangered practices. Volunteers running 4,000-person festivals unpaid. Judged by an independent panel of First Nations and diaspora cultural leaders.
>
> What we can offer a partner that is unusual in this category: **measured reach into specific cultural communities** — verified attendance from digital check-in at the door, reported by community and suburb. Not estimated attendance and not a photo of a banner.
>
> I should be straight with you: we launch in September, so there is no audience yet. FY27 is priced as inaugural-year risk, and the founding partner position only exists once.
>
> Would 20 minutes in the next fortnight be useful? Happy to send the prospectus first if you'd prefer to read before talking.
>
> Best regards,
>
> **Bibin Jose**
> Founder & CEO, CulturePass
> [phone] · cultureos.co@gmail.com

**Notes.** The pre-launch disclosure in paragraph five is doing real work — it is what makes inaugural-year pricing coherent rather than suspicious, and a sponsorship lead who later discovers you concealed it will not take the FY28 call.

## S8 — Renewal / QBR (FY28 onward)

**Subject:** `[Brand] x CulturePass — [Q] results and FY[X] options`

> Hi [First name],
>
> [Quarter] summary attached. Headlines:
>
> - **[N] verified attendances** at events in your category — [up/down] [X]% on last quarter
> - Reached **[N] distinct cultural communities** across [N] suburbs
> - [One notable host story or outcome]
> - **[Anything that underdelivered against what we contracted, stated plainly, with the make-good.]**
>
> Renewal window opens [month]. I'd like to propose [specific change based on what actually worked] rather than simply rolling over.
>
> Free [two specific times]?
>
> **Bibin**

**Notes.** The fourth bullet is mandatory whenever something underdelivered. Disclosing a shortfall before the sponsor finds it is the single largest driver of renewal in sponsorship, and concealing one is the single largest driver of non-renewal.
