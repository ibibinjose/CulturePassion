# CulturePass — Funding Strategy

**August 2026** · Owner: CEO · Reviewed monthly, re-forecast quarterly.

---

## 1. The blended-capital thesis

Three capital sources, each doing a job the others cannot:

| Source | FY27–FY29 | What it buys | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Equity** | A$2.5M seed (+ ~A$4.5M gated Series A) | Speed, engineering, and the ability to lose money deliberately while building supply | Dilution |
| **Non-dilutive grants** | A$2.55M target | CAC in communities where commercial CAC never pays back — which are exactly the communities that constitute the moat | Reporting obligations, application time, alignment constraints |
| **Commercial contracts** | A$4.56M (sponsorship + civic) | Revenue that largely arrives in advance of delivery, so it functions as working capital | Delivery obligations, concentration risk |

**The insight that makes this a strategy rather than a shopping list:** the communities with the highest strategic value — small diaspora associations, First Nations organisations, places of worship, newly-arrived community groups — have the *lowest* commercial value per host. On pure venture economics you would never spend A$210 acquiring a host who will pay you nothing. But those hosts are the community graph, and the community graph is the only durable moat.

Grant capital resolves that contradiction. Public and philanthropic funders want precisely that outcome and will pay for it. So grant money buys the asset that equity money could not justify buying.

## 2. Equity — A$2.5M seed

### The round

| | |
|---|---|
| Amount | A$2.5M |
| Instrument | Priced seed round or convertible note, per lead investor preference |
| Runway | FY27 in full plus H1 FY28 |
| Use | 45% product & engineering · 35% growth & community · 20% operations & compliance |

### Investor profile, in priority order

1. **Australian pre-seed/seed funds with a marketplace thesis.** Understand two-sided cold-start problems; will interrogate density and retention rather than TAM.
2. **Impact and civic-tech funds.** Value the measurement layer and the public-sector revenue line, which generalist funds tend to underweight.
3. **Angels with arts, migration or local-government backgrounds.** Bring doors that money cannot. A single angel with council relationships is worth more than the cheque.
4. **Generalist seed funds.** Fine, but expect the A$58M SAM to be the whole conversation.

### Positioning discipline

- Lead with the bottom-up A$58M Australian SAM, not the trillion-dollar context numbers. Sophisticated investors are testing whether you know the difference.
- Volunteer the sponsorship concentration risk and the pre-revenue status before being asked.
- Present the gated Series A as a feature. "We will not raise into weak retention" is the strongest sentence available to a founder in a seed meeting.

### What we will not do

- Take money that requires abandoning free-RSVP pricing
- Take money that requires selling individual-level user data
- Take a term sheet that removes the Cultural Advisory Council's binding authority on cultural matters
- Raise the Series A if the FY27 gates are missed

Each of these has a plausible short-term financial case and each would end the company's position in this category. They are decided in advance so they are not re-litigated under pressure in a live negotiation.

## 3. Non-dilutive — A$2.55M grant target

### By year and category

| Category | FY27 | FY28 | FY29 | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Arts & cultural development | 60,000 | 250,000 | 400,000 | 710,000 |
| Multicultural affairs & social cohesion | 80,000 | 300,000 | 450,000 | 830,000 |
| Tourism & visitor economy | 0 | 120,000 | 250,000 | 370,000 |
| Digital inclusion & capability (philanthropic) | 40,000 | 130,000 | 200,000 | 370,000 |
| **R&D Tax Incentive** | 70,000 | 100,000 | 100,000 | 270,000 |
| **Total** | **250,000** | **900,000** | **1,400,000** | **2,550,000** |

### Why the R&D Tax Incentive comes first

It is the only line that is not a competitive round. A refundable offset on eligible development expenditure for a company under the turnover threshold — apply, substantiate, receive. **Register in Q1 FY27.** It requires the Pty Ltd (a tax-exempt charity is not an eligible R&D entity), and it requires contemporaneous record-keeping of eligible activities from day one, which is a discipline to establish before there is anything to record.

### Pipeline discipline

| Rule | Rationale |
|---|---|
| **3× pipeline coverage** against each year's target | Grant win rates of 20–30% are normal. Coverage below 3× means the target is arithmetic fiction. |
| Fit score ≥3/5 or do not apply | A weak application costs 40 hours and damages the relationship for the next round |
| Verify every deadline at the funder's own source before starting | Program dates move; annual rounds mean a miss costs 12 months |
| Submit ≥3 days before deadline | Portal failures on deadline day are routine |
| Second reader on every submission | The control against over-claiming |
| **100% on-time acquittal** | A late acquittal costs future eligibility across an entire funding body |
| No claim DAT cannot evidence | An unsubstantiable acquittal ends the funder relationship permanently |

### The structural constraint, and how FY27 works around it

A significant share of philanthropic capital in Australia — private ancillary funds and most large trusts — can legally only distribute to **DGR Item 1** entities. A Pty Ltd is not eligible, no matter how strong the application.

FY27 solution: **auspicing** through an existing DGR1 partner, at a typical 5–10% administration fee. FY28 solution: form the Culture Passion Foundation. Full analysis, triggers and cautions: [`08-entity-and-foundation-strategy.md`](08-entity-and-foundation-strategy.md).

## 4. Commercial — sponsorship and civic contracts

A$4.56M modelled across FY27–FY29. Revenue, not funding — but it behaves like capital because it is contracted in advance of delivery.

| | FY27 | FY28 | FY29 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sponsorship & featured | 120,000 | 620,000 | 2,100,000 |
| Civic & tourism licences | 0 | 180,000 | 660,000 |
| Awards sponsorship | 0 | 260,000 | 900,000 |

**The FY27 prize:** two anchor sponsors would fund the entire Culture Passion Awards program and remove A$95,000 from the burn. The awards sponsorship line is modelled at A$0 in FY27 deliberately — it is upside, not plan.

**The concentration discipline:** minimum four independent national partners, no single sponsor above 15% of platform revenue, 18-month minimum terms. Sponsorship is 38% of FY29 revenue and a 50% shortfall costs nearly twice what a collapse in paid-ticket share costs.

### The downside path, costed

If the FY27 gates are missed: Melbourne only, headcount holds at 9, expansion stops, and the business runs toward grant-plus-sponsorship breakeven at roughly A$1.6M annual burn. Survivable on the seed plus a small bridge. **A smaller company, not a dead one** — and a materially better outcome than a Series A raised into a broken retention loop.

## 7. What each funder type actually needs to see

The same business, three genuinely different documents. Sending the wrong one is the most common and most avoidable failure in a blended-capital raise.

| | Investors | Government & foundations | Sponsors |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Their question** | Can this return 10×? | Does this deliver public value we are mandated to produce? | Can I reach this audience and prove it internally? |
| **Lead with** | Bottom-up SAM, unit economics, the gated Series A | The measurement gap and policy alignment | Verified reach and audience composition |
| **They discount** | Mission language without economics | Growth language without evidence of public benefit | Vision without measurement |
| **What wins** | Naming your own biggest risk | Governance, privacy, safeguarding, acquittal rigour | Editorial independence and the privacy floor |
| **Deck** | [`../05-pitch-deck/01-investor-deck.md`](../05-pitch-deck/01-investor-deck.md) | [`../05-pitch-deck/03-government-deck.md`](../05-pitch-deck/03-government-deck.md) | [`../05-pitch-deck/02-sponsor-deck.md`](../05-pitch-deck/02-sponsor-deck.md) |

**The one thing all three share:** state plainly what is not yet true. Pre-revenue disclosed upfront buys credibility for everything else; pre-revenue discovered in diligence discounts everything else.
