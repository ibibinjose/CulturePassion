# CulturePass — Australian Grant & Funding Target List

**As at August 2026** · Owner: CEO · Review monthly.

> ## ⚠ VERIFY BEFORE USE
> **Every amount, deadline, eligibility rule and program name below must be confirmed at the funder's own website before any work begins.** Australian grant programs are renamed, restructured, paused and re-scoped frequently — the Australia Council became Creative Australia, the Register of Cultural Organisations was absorbed into ATO administration in January 2024, and state programs change with budget cycles. Amounts shown are **indicative ranges for pipeline sizing only**. They are not quotes. Treat this document as a research map, not a source of facts.

**Fit score** is out of 30 per the framework in [`02-grant-application-framework.md`](02-grant-application-framework.md). Anything below 18 is not pursued.

## 4. Auspice partner candidates (FY27)

Needed because most philanthropic capital can only flow to DGR Item 1 entities. See [`08-entity-and-foundation-strategy.md`](08-entity-and-foundation-strategy.md) §5.

| Type | Why suitable | Approach |
|---|---|---|
| **Victorian ethnic community councils / multicultural peak bodies** | DGR-endorsed, mission-aligned, and already on the FY27 partnership target list for entirely separate reasons | Lead with the free-Professional-tier-for-members offer, then raise auspicing |
| **Australian Communities Foundation** | Established auspicing and sub-fund infrastructure | Direct enquiry |
| **Community foundations (Victorian)** | Local grant-making and auspicing capability | Direct enquiry |
| **Arts service organisations with DGR1** | Sector-aligned, understand the acquittal burden | Via arts peak bodies |
| **Cultural Infusion Foundation** | Same city, adjacent sector, established foundation structure. **Check ACNC register and ABN Lookup for actual charity subtype and DGR status first.** | Partnership conversation first, auspicing second |

**Typical auspice fee: 5–10% of the grant.** Cheap relative to A$15–40k of standing charity compliance, and it deepens a partnership you wanted anyway.

## 6. Tracking discipline

Maintain a single tracker with: funder, program, amount, deadline **verified at source with the date verified**, fit score, status, owner, next action, next action date, and outcome with feedback.

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| Rolling 12-month deadline calendar | Annual rounds mean a missed window costs a year |
| Re-verify every deadline quarterly | Programs move, pause and get renamed |
| Log every decision not to apply, with reason | Stops the same weak opportunity being re-litigated each quarter |
| Request feedback on every unsuccessful application | The cheapest improvement available; most funders will give it |
| Record every funder relationship contact | Most funders fund the same organisations repeatedly — the second application is always easier |
| Never let an acquittal go late | One late acquittal can close an entire funding body to you |
