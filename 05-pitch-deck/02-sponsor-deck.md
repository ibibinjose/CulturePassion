**8 slides · 15 minutes** · Companion to [`../04-culture-passion-awards/04-sponsorship-prospectus.md`](../04-culture-passion-awards/04-sponsorship-prospectus.md)

The sponsor conversation is different from the investor conversation in one critical way: **the person across the table needs to justify this internally.** Your job is to give them the argument they will make to their boss, not to impress them personally.

---

> # CulturePass

> **The Culture Passion Awards · Founding Partner opportunity**

**Notes.** Open with their brand's own multicultural commitment, named specifically. If you cannot name it from their published material, you have not done the research and should reschedule.

## Slide 2 — The audience problem they already have

> ### You have a multicultural marketing budget and no reliable way to spend it
>
> - Paid social targets language and interest proxies, not cultural participation
> - Community sponsorship reaches one festival, one weekend, one community
> - Attendance is estimated by the organiser, and nobody checks
> - Audience composition is unknown, so the report to your board is a photograph and a guess
>
> *You are asked to prove community impact. Nothing available to you produces evidence.*

**Notes.** This is the slide that earns the meeting's attention. Do not pitch yet — describe their problem accurately and stop talking.

## Slide 4 — What you can measure

> ### Verified, not estimated
>
> | | Ordinary sponsorship | CulturePass |
> |---|---|---|
> | Attendance | Organiser's estimate | Signed digital pass, scanned at the gate |
> | Audience composition | Unknown | Reported by cultural community, aggregate |
> | Geography | Venue postcode | Suburb-level participation and dispersal |
> | Reporting | A photo and a thank-you | Monthly data report, quarterly review |
> | Duration | One weekend | Year-round |
>
> **Privacy floor:** aggregate only, small-cell suppression, **individual-level data never shared.** No exception, no premium tier.

**Notes.** The privacy floor will disappoint a performance marketer and reassure a brand or CSR lead. Say it early and without apology — a partner who needs individual-level targeting is not a fit, and finding that out in meeting one saves everyone a quarter.

## Slide 5 — The Culture Passion Awards

> ### Recognition for people who have never received any
>
> Four categories. Unrestricted grants up to A$5,000. Professional documentation of each recipient's work, theirs to keep.
>
> - **Heritage Guardian** — elders preserving endangered practices
> - **Community Vanguard** — volunteer festival organisers operating at scale
> - **Youth Cultural Pioneer** — under-30 innovators
> - **Global Bridge** — genuine cross-community collaboration
>
> Judged by an independent panel of First Nations and diaspora cultural leaders. **Sponsors have no vote.**

**Notes.** "Sponsors have no vote" is a selling point, not a limitation. A sponsor whose logo sits on an award with contested independence has bought a liability. Frame it that way.

## Slide 7 — The rules you are agreeing to

> ### These are why the platform is worth sponsoring
>
> - Sponsored placement is **labelled** and does not influence organic ranking. "For You" is not purchasable.
> - Sponsors have **no vote, no veto, no advance sight** of award recipients.
> - Prize money is paid **directly to recipients**.
> - Cultural imagery in any activation requires a **partnering community host** and Advisory Council review.
> - **No individual-level data.** Ever.
> - Free events stay free. Sponsorship does not change that.

**Notes.** Present this as a feature. The credibility of the surface is the entire product they are buying; a feed audiences distrust reaches nobody, and a partner who understands that will value the constraint.

## Slide 8 — The ask, and what we are not claiming

> ### Founding Partner, inaugural year
>
> **What is true:** the platform is built and working. The awards are designed, governed and budgeted. The pilot launches September 2026.
>
> **What is not true:** there is no audience yet. No paying customers, no signed sponsors, no launched pilot. Every audience figure here is a projection from a model we will share in full, including its downside cases.
>
> FY27 is priced as inaugural-year risk. A partner who wants proven reach should talk to us in FY28. **The founding position is only available now.**
>
> *Next step: a 20-minute conversation.*

**Notes.** Volunteering the pre-launch status is not a weakness in a sponsorship conversation — it is what allows the inaugural-year pricing to make sense. A sponsor who discovers it later feels misled; a sponsor told upfront is being offered a legible risk-reward trade.
