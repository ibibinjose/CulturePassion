# CulturePass — Government & Civic Deck

**9 slides · 20 minutes** · For councils, state cultural and multicultural bodies, tourism boards, and grant assessors.

**The single most important rule for this audience:** they are not buying software and they are not funding innovation. **They are buying the answer to a question their minister, council or board has already asked them and which they currently cannot answer.** Find that question in the first five minutes and spend the rest of the meeting on it.

## Slide 2 — The measurement gap

> ### Four questions you are asked, and cannot currently answer
>
> 1. How many people participated in the cultural programs we funded this year?
> 2. Which communities are we under-serving relative to their population share?
> 3. Did funded activity draw people beyond the CBD?
> 4. **Is participation growing, or are we reaching the same people repeatedly?**
>
> *Question 4 has no instrument anywhere in Australia. Longitudinal repeat-participation data at community granularity does not exist.*

**Notes.** Ask which of the four they were most recently asked. Their answer determines the rest of the meeting. Question 4 is almost always the one that lands, because it is the one that makes an annual report indefensible.

---

## Slide 3 — Why the gap exists

> ### It is not a diligence failure. There is no instrument.
>
> - Grassroots and multicultural cultural activity runs on WhatsApp, closed Facebook groups, and printed flyers
> - Organisers count attendance by estimate on the night, because they have no alternative
> - Ticketing platforms are priced for A$80 tickets and are absent from a A$12 community event
> - So the data you receive is self-reported, unverifiable, and not comparable between organisations or across years

## Slide 5 — The four metrics

> | Metric | Definition |
> |---|---|
> | **Community engagement** | Verified attendances segmented by cultural community |
> | **Economic impact** | Gross revenue generated for small-scale and non-profit hosts |
> | **Cultural representation** | Distinct cultural, linguistic and First Nations communities active |
> | **Geographic dispersal** | Attendance by suburb; share outside the CBD |
>
> Verified check-ins and estimated attendance are **always reported as distinct figures**, with the estimation method stated.

**Notes.** That last line is what distinguishes a measurement instrument from a marketing dashboard, and a data-literate public servant will notice it.

## Slide 7 — Governance, privacy and safeguarding

> ### Built to be procurable
>
> | | |
> |---|---|
> | Privacy | Australian Privacy Act / APPs; privacy impact assessment pre-launch; AU data residency |
> | Data ethics | **Individual-level data never sold.** Aggregate reporting only, small-cell suppression. |
> | First Nations data | Indigenous Data Sovereignty; communities control use of data about their activity |
> | Cultural governance | **Paid Cultural Advisory Council with binding authority** on cultural matters |
> | Safeguarding | Victorian Child Safe Standards; host attestations; insurance verification |
> | Accessibility | WCAG 2.2 AA, externally audited |
> | Consumer law | ACL-compliant ticketing, refund and disclosure terms |
> | Security | PCI-DSS SAQ-A; no card data on our infrastructure; signed-token pass validation |

**Notes.** This slide exists to get through procurement, and its absence is the most common reason a promising civic pilot stalls. If they linger on any row, that row is their blocker — offer the underlying document.
