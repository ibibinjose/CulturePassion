02 — The problem

Cultural participation is large, real, and invisible to infrastructure

Participants

Discovery requires already being inside the community. WhatsApp broadcast lists, closed Facebook groups, flyers in grocery shops. If you are not in, there is no way in.

Organisers

Mainstream ticketing fees are calibrated for A$80 tickets and consume 15–20% of a A$12 one. So: cash at the door, a spreadsheet, no pre-sale, no data, no evidence for the next grant.

Funders

Councils and multicultural bodies distribute significant public money and receive back attendance estimates written on the night. There is no instrument.

Three problems, one platform. None is solvable alone — participants will not use an empty feed, organisers will not publish to no audience, funders will not licence thin coverage.

The wedge: make the single best cultural discovery feed for one metro area, and organisers come because that is where the audience is.
03 — The solution
Built · working · not a mockup

Discover, join, organise, measure

Discover

City feed, natural-language search ("free Malayalam events this weekend"), map, cultural calendar — organised by community, not by a generic "Arts & Culture" category.

Join

Free RSVP and low-fee ticketing. Digital passes with signed QR codes. Gate check-in that blocks duplicate, cancelled and wrong-event scans.

Organise

Event wizard, guest lists, co-hosts, verified badges, community and business profiles — and grant acquittal reports.

Measure

First-party analytics producing the participation, representation and dispersal data that nobody currently supplies to anyone.

39 frontend routes · 15 API modules · 11 database schema groups · AWS CDK (CloudFront, ALB, Fargate, Aurora Serverless v2, Cognito, SQS, SES) · Stripe · signed-token QR validation · all packages typecheck and build clean

05 — Product & the wedge

Discovery is the wedge. Ticketing is a feature.

The first thing that must be true: CulturePass is the best answer to "what cultural thing is on near me this weekend." Everything else is downstream of that.

  • FY27 supply acquisition is not gated on willingness to pay. We onboard hosts publishing free events aggressively, because feed density is the product.
  • Free RSVP events are free forever — architecturally, not as a pricing promise. A free RSVP never touches a payment path, so it cannot accrue a fee.
  • Then retention — through the grant acquittal report. Once a host's funded application cites our data, switching costs become real.
  • Then the civic product — the measurement layer, which is the smallest revenue line and the largest source of long-term enterprise value.
The obvious challenge, answered: monetising free events would double short-term revenue and end the company's legitimacy. Free events are the majority of cultural life and the reason hosts arrive at all.

The risk we name before you find it

Sponsorship is 38% of FY29 revenue. A 50% sponsorship shortfall costs A$1.05M — nearly twice what a total collapse in paid-ticket share costs. The largest vulnerability in this model is not the thing the company looks like it does. Mitigation: minimum four independent national partners, no single partner above 15% of revenue, 18-month minimum terms.

Gross margin moves 66% → 86%. That is mix shift toward sponsorship and licences, not efficiency gains. Anyone reading it as operating leverage has misread it.
07 — Traction

What is true, and what is not

True today

Production platform built end to end. Purpose-built for Melbourne — diaspora-language search working, city already the default. Culture Passion Awards designed, governed and budgeted. Pilot launches September 2026 across six suburbs.

Not true today

No paying customers. No signed sponsors. No committed grants. No launched pilot.

Every figure in this deck is a planning assumption traced to an explicit driver. The drivers are the thing to challenge.

This slide is an asset, not a liability. An investor who discovers pre-revenue status by cross-examination discounts everything else; an investor told plainly extends credibility to the rest of the deck.
